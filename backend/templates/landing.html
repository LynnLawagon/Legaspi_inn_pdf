About

This OCR system extracts text from National IDs and School IDs, automatically detecting important fields such as First Name, Middle Name, Last Name, Address, and Date of Birth. The extracted information can be reviewed and edited by the user before being organized and saved as a PDF report, making record-keeping simple and efficient.

How to use AIntelli?

Upload an image of the ID or capture a photo using the camera feature.

The system processes the image and extracts text automatically.

Recalculate if age is unmatch.

Extracted information is displayed on-screen for review.

Save the results as a PDF, which will be stored in the designated folder.

History of Legaspi Inn

Legaspi Inn began as a small lodging business originally owned and operated by an older family member of the current owners. As the former owner aged, the responsibility of managing the inn was handed over to the family of the current owner. Since then, the family has taken an active role in overseeing the daily operations to ensure that the inn is well-maintained, services are delivered properly, and staff needs are addressed.

Under the current management, Legaspi Inn has continued to provide affordable and clean accommodation, with a focus on simplicity, comfort, and personalized service. The owners and co-owners work closely with staff to monitor the inn’s operations, from guest check-ins to room maintenance and inventory control.

To further strengthen operational efficiency and improve the guest verification process, AIntelli was developed as an internal ID scanning and documentation system for Legaspi Inn. By automating ID data extraction, age validation, and PDF report generation, AIntelli supports the inn’s commitment to organized record-keeping, faster check-ins, and reliable guest management.

How AIntelli Supports
Legaspi Inn?

  2. Helps staff validate guest eligibility.
  3. Stores verified guest details securely.
  4. Faster check-in process.
  5. Generates PDF documentation reports.
